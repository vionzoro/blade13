## Description

```
UNICORN, the city’s rainbow-energy AI, has corrupted every machine into a prism beast. A standing chibi unicorn cuts back with one rainbow monomolecular blade, collects seven-color shards, and triggers PRISM BREAK before the Black Unicorn ends the run.

You attack automatically — positioning is the whole game. Blink through a chain of enemies to escape or execute, but it runs on charges and landing costs you a beat of attack recovery, so it is a decision rather than a button. Kills fill your PRISM BREAK meter, and it does NOT fire on its own: you pick the moment. While it burns, the screen tears into chroma split and every kill extends it; the mechanics are unchanged from the original burst system.

Every level offers three cards from a pool you will only ever see a third of. PROGRAM and CHROME are clean upgrades. OVERLOAD always costs you something — +55% damage for -30% max HP, +50% attack speed for -25% reach. Some cards only appear once you have committed to a line.

Seven enemy families, elites, telegraphed bosses, and hives that keep spawning until you go and deal with them. The threat level climbs every 22 seconds, the first Black Unicorn arrives at 32 seconds, and enemy speed keeps rising until running stops working. Spike traps retract and extend across a city that generates itself from a coordinate hash — infinite in every direction, with nothing stored.

Everything is drawn with Canvas 2D commands and synthesised at runtime. No images, no audio files, no external requests.

PLAY ON MOBILE: portrait is auto-detected and one thumb does everything — drag anywhere to move, tap to blink.

Desktop: WASD to move, SPACE to blink, F to trigger PRISM BREAK, 1/2/3 to pick a card.
```
