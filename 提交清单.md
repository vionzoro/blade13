## Description ⚠️ 主题公布后在开头补一句主题关联

```
A lone runner with three monomolecular blades, dropped into a neon city that never stops sending machines.

You attack automatically — positioning is the whole game. Blink through a chain of enemies to escape or execute, but it runs on charges and landing costs you a beat of attack recovery, so it is a decision rather than a button. Kills fill your Overclock meter, and it does NOT fire on its own: you pick the moment. While it burns, the screen tears into chroma split and every kill extends it.

Every level offers three cards from a pool you will only ever see a third of. PROGRAM and CHROME are clean upgrades. OVERLOAD always costs you something — +55% damage for -30% max HP, +50% attack speed for -25% reach. Some cards only appear once you have committed to a line.

Seven enemy families, elites, telegraphed bosses, and hives that keep spawning until you go and deal with them. The threat level climbs every 28 seconds and enemy speed keeps rising until running stops working. Spike traps retract and extend across a city that generates itself from a coordinate hash — infinite in every direction, with nothing stored.

Everything is drawn with Canvas 2D commands and synthesised at runtime. No images, no audio files, no external requests.

PLAY ON MOBILE: portrait is auto-detected and one thumb does everything — drag anywhere to move, tap to blink.

Desktop: WASD to move, SPACE to blink, F to overclock, 1/2/3 to pick a card.
```
